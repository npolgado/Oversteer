- [ ] Notably harder to outrun than chasers at the same wave

**`drifter-feel`** — Score=1500, pure drifters.
- [ ] Drifters visually drift sideways every 2–3.5s
- [ ] Encircling during a drift arc is harder (hitbox shifts)
- [ ] Waiting for the straight phase makes encirclement easier

**`blocker-trail-contest`** — Score=2000, pure blockers, wider_trail.
- [ ] Blockers move to the centroid of your trail (midpoint, not the endpoint)
- [ ] They reposition when you build a new trail segment in a different direction
- [ ] Wider trail makes their parking more effective (harder to loop around them)
- [ ] Strategy: displace them by forcing a detour, then loop from the other side

**`flanker-fight`** — Score=2500, pure flankers.
- [ ] Flankers approach perpendicular to your velocity (to the side, not head-on)
- [ ] Charge triggers when <120px from player — visually they lunge
- [ ] If you change direction, their approach angle adjusts (perpendicular to NEW heading)
- [ ] Fastest enemy type in the pool (470 speed) — feel vs chasers (420)

**`bomber-hazard-test`** — Score=3000, pure bombers.

- [ ] Hazard zone lingers (CFG.BOMB_ZONE_DURATION) — note how long

**`elite-stress`** — Wave 5, score=2500, organic elite mix.

- [ ] Elite takes 2 encirclement hits — first hit does NOT kill it

## Open Questions / Balance Notes

_(Filled in from 2026-06-01 session)_

- **Intermittent instant wave-end** — observed in `elite-stress` and `endgame-chaos`. Likely boss spawn logic incorrectly triggering wave_end condition on load. Root cause unknown; needs investigation.
- **Bomb one-shots boss** — verify whether CFG.BOSS_HP vs bomb damage values are correctly balanced. Probably not intentional.
- **Boss design needs a rework** — all patterns feel awkward to engage with. Unpredictable movement, no clear window for encirclement. Core priority for next balance pass.
- **Trail burn: combo + VFX** — burn kills must increment combo; each tick and kill needs burn particle feedback.
- **Chain lightning readability** — arc FX is too subtle to notice during gameplay. Needs a brighter/longer visual and a distinct audio cue.
- **Biomes need gameplay depth** — color tint and fog overlay are imperceptible and insufficient. Each biome needs a unique mechanical element (hazard, obstacle, weather) that changes strategy.
- **Pickup icon visibility** — icons blend into city biome background. Need glow, outline, or pulse animation.
- **Situation tester gaps identified this session:**
  - Need: skip directly to upgrade break UI with N free rerolls (for shop + bias testing)
  - Need: `waveProgress` parameter to trigger mid-wave events (horde) immediately
  - Need: scatter physical scrap tokens as collectibles (for magnet testing) — `scrap:` sets currency, not tokens
  - Need: `bossDefeated` scenarios should open directly at wave_end / upgrade break
- **General positives:** time slow pickup is excellent; upgrade icons look clean; music has no regression; new pickups well-received overall.
