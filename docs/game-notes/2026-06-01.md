## Phase 2: Enemy Behaviors

**`interceptor-predict`** — Score=1000, pure interceptors.
- [ ] Interceptors aim AHEAD of you — change direction sharply and watch them overshoot
- [ ] Notably harder to outrun than chasers at the same wave
- [ ] Confirm lead-targeting: at low player speed (<50px/s) they should fall back to direct chase

**`drifter-feel`** — Score=1500, pure drifters.
- [ ] Drifters visually drift sideways every 2–3.5s
- [ ] Encircling during a drift arc is harder (hitbox shifts)
- [ ] Waiting for the straight phase makes encirclement easier
- [ ] Drift timer is per-enemy — stagger is visible in a group

**`blocker-trail-contest`** — Score=2000, pure blockers, wider_trail.
- [ ] Blockers move to the centroid of your trail (midpoint, not the endpoint)
- [ ] They reposition when you build a new trail segment in a different direction
- [ ] Wider trail makes their parking more effective (harder to loop around them)
- [ ] Strategy: displace them by forcing a detour, then loop from the other side

**`flanker-fight`** — Score=2500, pure flankers.
- [ ] Flankers approach perpendicular to your velocity (to the side, not head-on)
- [ ] Charge triggers when <120px from player — visually they lunge
- [ ] If you change direction, their approach angle adjusts (perpendicular to NEW heading)
- [ ] Fastest enemy type in the pool (470 speed) — feel vs chasers (420)

**`bomber-hazard-test`** — Score=3000, pure bombers.
- [ ] Bombers orbit ~300px ahead of player

- [ ] Hazard zone lingers (CFG.BOMB_ZONE_DURATION) — note how long

**`elite-stress`** — Wave 5, score=2500, organic elite mix.
