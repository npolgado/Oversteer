## 6. Bugs / Unexpected Behavior

| # | Description | Repro | Severity |
|---|-------------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Hex pickups spawn on top of rock props | Observed wave 4–5, natural play | Low — cosmetic/minor |
| 2 | Drift "explode forward" on wave 6 — holding drift + tight turn seemed to suddenly launch the car forward at high speed | Run 1, wave 6: Drift King + Afterburner + Nitro Drift + Tight Turns 'R', holding drift and steering hard circle | Medium — may be upgrade interaction or physics edge case |
| 3 | ~~End screen stats broken — Enemies Killed = 0, Peak Combo = 0, Near Miss = 0 despite active gameplay.~~ **FIXED** — `updateRunStats` was never called after near-miss or encirclement events; only drift_tick was wired. Added two calls in `gameLoop.ts:_tickCombat` and `_tickTrail`. | Run 1, wave 6 death, all-modifiers | High — **resolved** |

**Bug 2 detail:** Possibly a velocity accumulation issue when Nitro Drift + Afterburner + Drift King all fire simultaneously during a sustained tight drift. Could also be the Afterburner triggering at end-of-drift and combining with existing drift momentum. Needs isolated repro without all modifiers.

**Bug 3 detail (resolved):** `updateRunStats` in `pureLogic.ts` handles `near_miss`, `encircle`, and `drift_tick` event types. Only `drift_tick` was being emitted (via `updateScoring` in `scoring.ts`). The `near_miss` and `encircle` calls were missing from `gameLoop.ts`, so kill count, near-miss count, and peak combo never accumulated. Fix: added `updateRunStats(..., { type: 'near_miss', ... })` in `_tickCombat` and `updateRunStats(..., { type: 'encircle', killCount, ... })` in `_tickTrail`.
