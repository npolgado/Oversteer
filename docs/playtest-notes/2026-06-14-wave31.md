# Playtest Notes — Wave 31 Run (2026-06-14)

Platform: TS/Pixi port (`npm run dev`). Build: commit `5c206b5` (Phase 4.9 Core Feel Lock).

---

## Raw Observations

### Difficulty / Scaling
- Run is beatable **fully AFK** by wave 26+. Enemy count flatlines at wave 5; waves 22 and 30 feel identical to wave 5 density (3–4 enemies on screen).
- Trail burn trivially kills wave-30 enemies (one-shot); a stationary spiral persists forever and acts as free AFK defense.
- Boss HP is flat — wave-30 boss dies as fast as wave-5 boss.
- Horde wave 16 felt weaker than a normal combat wave's cumulative kill count.
- Auto-repair (3×) may out-heal damage once other levers are tuned.

### Bugs Observed
- **Music cuts out** on biome transition (wave 8 → silent until wave 15); wave count-in stalls intermittently.
- **Pause shows "Music 10%"** but music is actually silent / much quieter than displayed.
- **Gamepad can't select upgrades** on level-up screen (intermittent); reroll works, A/X/Y card selection does not.
- **Horde camera** not centered on player when near map edges; player can drift off-screen.
- **Pickups spawn inside obstacles** (rocks, pillars).
- **Bomb pickup** has no detonation feedback (no flash, no shockwave ring).
- **Combo banner caps at "COMBO 8X"** — combos 9–16 are silent.
- **Drift multiplier bar** (bottom-left) shows a cryptic 1-second tick indicator instead of combo level.
- **Event log** unreadable mid-action; no pickup label at collection point.
- **False "BLANK SCREEN"** error fires during pause due to dimmed overlay tripping the render probe.
- **Wind lines** trigger too late (threshold 0.7 → starts at 70% speed, feels dead at moderate speed).
- **Owned upgrades list** (upgrade screen) is one unreadable horizontal string.

### World Geometry
- 4.9 grey-circle pillars and biome structures were not wanted; blocked movement without adding fun.
- Boss wave has no arena feel — same open 3000×3000 world.

## Backlog (Deferred — Future Phase)

### Upgrade System
- **Rarity tiers** — common / uncommon / rare / legendary; upgrade pool weighted by tier.
- **Upgrade synergies** — diesel (speed × damage) and action-man (combo × repair) cross-upgrade bonuses.
- **Button-triggered active items** — turret (places auto-targeting turret), slow-mo (brief bullet-time), shockwave (radial push).
- **Shop expansion** — more items, reroll improvements, price scaling.

### Boss Design
- **Ranged attacks** — projectile patterns on later boss tiers.
- **Weak points** — boss exposes a glowing weak point mid-phase; bonus damage window.

### Art / Audio
- **Asset overhaul** — replace placeholder sprites with final art.
- **Biome music variety** — more tracks per biome to reduce repetition.

### Auto-repair balance
- With enemy damage/count fixed (2.1–2.4), re-evaluate whether 3× auto-repair (15 HP/s) out-heals. If yes, cap regen or tune tier cost.
